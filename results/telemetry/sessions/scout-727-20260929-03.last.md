For the eventual comparison, our exact normalization is:

\[
n=md,\qquad
\Delta_{a,b}=a n^a\bigl(\varepsilon^b+q^{-b}+2^{-bn}\bigr).
\]

Equivalently, with \(\ell=\log_2q\),

\[
\Delta_{a,b}=a n^a
  \bigl(\varepsilon^b+2^{-b\ell}+2^{-bn}\bigr).
\]

Here \(q\) is the **field cardinality**, \(m\) is the polynomial dimension, and the number of extracted \(q\)-dimensional EPR pairs is \(2^m\). These quantities must not be interchanged.

Our Pauli headline bounds an **unsquared state norm** and **sums of squared operator-action norms**. Operator answers are summed, not averaged. LDT bounds an off-diagonal consistency defect, which is at most one on the normalized bipartite state. A benchmark using squared state distance, a half-normalized measurement distance, or root-mean-square operator distance requires an explicit conversion before comparing exponents.

Our QPBT domain has \(d\ge1\), \(q=2^\ell\) with \(\ell\) odd, and \(m\mid q\), hence \(m\ge1\). The field model is fixed. Here is a conservative derivation, including both point and global consistency. Put

\[
X=10000k^2m^4
 \left[\varepsilon^{1/1024}+(d/q)^{1/1024}
       +e^{-k/(80000m^2)}\right],
\qquad t=X^{1/32}.
\]

The current induction estimate gives \(\sigma\le X\). Subadditivity of the \(1/32\)-power and \(10000^{1/32}\le2\) give

\[
100t\le\nu_{\rm new}.
\]

Work in the only interesting branch \(\nu_{\rm new}<1\). Then \(t<1/100\), and the same branch forces \(\varepsilon\le1\) and \(d/q\le1\). Moreover \(\varepsilon\le X\) and \(md/q\le X\). Consequently,

\[
\zeta_1=2\sigma+2\sqrt{3\varepsilon+2\sigma}+md/q
 \le8\sqrt X,
\quad
\zeta_1^{1/8}\le2t^2,
\quad \zeta_1\le1.
\]

Retain the **actual** completion error, including its residual term:

\[
\zeta_2=200\zeta_1^{1/4}+40\zeta_1^{1/8}+2\zeta_1
 \le484t^2.
\]

The repaired line-169 error satisfies
\(\eta=\zeta_1+10\zeta_1^{1/8}\le22t^2\), and

\[
\zeta_3/2=3\zeta_1+3\zeta_2\le1458t^2.
\]

Thus the point-consistency error is at most

\[
2\sigma+2\sqrt{\eta+\zeta_3/2}
 \le2t+2\sqrt{1480}\,t
 \le80t,
\]

while global consistency is at most \(1458t^2\le100t\).
Both are bounded by \(\nu_{\rm new}\). In the complementary branch, normalized consistency is at most one.
