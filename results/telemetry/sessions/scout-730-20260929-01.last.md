### Verified comparisons

**Both earlier zero-error examples remain valid for \(F\), but for a different quantitative reason at the larger example.**

Take
\[
e=0,\qquad m=1,\qquad d=n,\qquad q=2^{n+1}.
\]
For \(n=2^{50}\) or \(2^{80}\), the extension degree \(n+1\) is odd, so these are admissible on both sides.

| Parameters | Verified inequalities |
|---|---|
| \(n=2^{50}\) | \(f=f_{\rm raw}=4\), while \(C<2^{-16776968}\) and \(F\le F_4<2^{-1073741589}\). |
| \(n=2^{80}\) | \(f\le f_{\rm raw}<2^{-2^{60}}<F\le F_4\le C\). |

Consequently, **neither \(C\), \(F\), nor \(F_4\) is uniformly ordered with \(f\)**. The same counterexamples work against \(f_{\rm raw}\).

Here are reproducible analytic certificates; they require no floating-point evaluation of tiny quantities.

For \(e=0\),
\[
T=5m^2\sqrt{2z}+z.
\]
If \(48n\le q\) and \(L\le2^{-40}\), then
\[
r,\ q^{-1}\le\sqrt L,\qquad
S\le21\sqrt L,\qquad X<2^{14}\sqrt L.
\]
Hence \(H<9/32<1\), and
\[
\sqrt\eta\le32L^{1/8},\qquad
\boxed{f\le2^{11}L^{1/8}}.
\]
The final coefficient check is
\[
66\cdot21+46+13\cdot32=1848<2^{11}.
\]

For \(n=2^k\), \(m=1\), \(q=2^{n+1}\), we have
\[
T\le16\sqrt z,\qquad z\le2^{k-n},
\]
so
\[
L<2^{\ell_k},\qquad
\ell_k=40+A(k+2)+\frac{4+k/2-n/2}{40000}.
\]
At \(k=80\), exact rational arithmetic verifies
\[
\ell_{80}<-40,\qquad
18+\ell_{80}/8<-2^{60}.
\]
Since \(104<2^7\), this proves the second row.

At \(k=50\), the retained public tail instead gives
\[
L>A(4n)^A2^{-n/10000}
>2^{52A-2^{50}/10000}>1.
\]
Thus \(H>1\) and the public definition returns \(4\). The local upper bounds follow from \(E_b<2\cdot2^{-bn}\).

**The retained field and tail rates really matter.** In the preceding small-\(L\), zero-error regime, root subadditivity gives
\[
f\le
2^{11}[A(4n)^A]^{1/8}
\left(
9^{\beta/8}m^{\beta/4}z^{\beta/16}
+q^{-\beta/8}
+2^{-\beta n/2}
\right).
\]
The surviving field and tail powers are therefore
\[
z^{1/640000},\qquad 2^{-n/80000},
\]
both faster than \(F\)'s common power \(1/1048576\).

For a separate tail example, take \(e=0,m=1,d=n=2^{60}\) and \(q=2^{16n+1}\). Then
\[
L<2^{40+62A-n/10000},
\]
and exact rational arithmetic gives
\[
f_{\rm raw}<2^{-2^{40}}<F.
\]
This exhibits the public tail advantage after the field contribution has been suppressed.

**The error exponent also has a genuine effect in the opposite direction.** The actual public formula implies, throughout the domain,
\[
f\ge e^{1/1280000}.
\]
Inside its regime, use \(T\ge e^{1/4}\), \(L\ge e^{\beta/4}\), \(\eta\ge H\), and \(J\ge L^{1/8}\); outside, the cap is \(4\).

Thus, at \(m=1,d=n=2^{80},q=2^{n+1}\), changing only \(e\) to \(2^{-n}\) gives
\[
F\le F_4
<2^{355-2^{60}}
<2^{-n/1280000}
\le f.
\]
The ordering therefore reverses relative to the zero-error example at the same \(m,d,q\).
